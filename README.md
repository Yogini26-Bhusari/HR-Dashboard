# HR-Dashboard

1. Project Title / Headline
HR Analytics Dashboard: Employee Attrition & Workforce Insights

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary trends, and job satisfaction patterns to support HR decision-making and organizational growth.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The HR Analytics Dashboard is a data-driven Power BI report developed to help organizations monitor employee attrition trends, workforce distribution, salary categories, and employee satisfaction levels through interactive visualizations.

This dashboard enables HR teams and business managers to identify key factors affecting employee retention and make informed workforce planning decisions.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used for building the dashboard and visual reports.
📂 Power Query – Used for cleaning, transforming, and preparing HR data.
🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures such as Attrition Rate and dynamic KPIs.
📝 Data Modeling – Structured HR dataset relationships for filtering and cross-analysis.
📈 Interactive Visualizations – KPI cards, donut charts, bar charts, area charts, funnel charts, and slicers.
📁 File Format – .pbit / .pbix for dashboard development and sharing.
4. Data Source

Source: HR Analytics Employee Dataset

The dataset contains employee-related organizational data, including:

Employee IDs
Department details
Age groups
Salary slabs
Job roles
Gender distribution
Job satisfaction ratings
Attrition records
Monthly income
Years at company

The data is structured to support employee attrition analysis and workforce insights across different departments and employee categories.

5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

Employee attrition is one of the major challenges faced by organizations, affecting productivity, recruitment costs, and business continuity.

HR departments often struggle to answer questions such as:

Which departments experience the highest attrition?
Which salary groups have more employee turnover?
How does job satisfaction impact attrition?
Which age groups are more likely to leave?
Which job roles are most affected?

Analyzing these trends manually becomes difficult when dealing with large HR datasets.

• Goal of the Dashboard

The main objective of the dashboard is to:

Monitor employee attrition patterns across the organization.
Identify key factors influencing employee turnover.
Analyze workforce demographics and satisfaction levels.
Support HR teams in improving employee retention strategies.
Provide interactive insights for workforce planning and management.
• Walkthrough of Key Visuals
Key KPI Cards

The dashboard displays important HR metrics such as:

Total Employees
Total Attrition Count
Attrition Rate
Total Monthly Income
Average Employee Age
Total Years at Company

These KPIs provide a quick overview of workforce health and attrition trends.

Attrition by Education (Donut Chart)

Shows employee attrition distribution across different education backgrounds.

Helps identify whether certain education levels experience higher employee turnover.

Attrition by Age Group (Column Chart)

Displays attrition trends among different employee age groups.

Useful for understanding which age categories are more likely to leave the organization.

Job Satisfaction Analysis (Matrix / Pivot Table)

Analyzes attrition count based on:

Job Roles
Job Satisfaction Ratings

This visual helps identify roles with lower satisfaction and higher attrition risk.

Attrition by Salary Slab (Bar Chart)

Shows employee turnover across various salary ranges.

Helps HR teams understand how compensation impacts employee retention.

Attrition by Job Role (Clustered Bar Chart)

Compares attrition levels among different job positions.

Useful for identifying departments or roles with critical turnover issues.

Attrition by Years at Company (Area Chart)

Displays how employee attrition changes with experience and tenure.

Helps identify whether new employees or experienced employees are leaving more frequently.

Gender-wise Attrition (Funnel Chart)

Analyzes attrition distribution based on gender.

Supports diversity analysis and workforce inclusion monitoring.

Department Filter (Slicer)

Interactive slicer allows users to filter the entire dashboard by department.

Enables detailed department-level analysis and comparisons.

• Business Impact & Insights
Employee Retention Strategy

HR teams can identify major causes of attrition and implement targeted retention programs.

Workforce Planning

Managers can analyze turnover trends and improve hiring and workforce allocation decisions.

Salary Optimization

Organizations can study the relationship between salary slabs and employee turnover to improve compensation strategies.

Employee Satisfaction Improvement

The dashboard helps identify low-satisfaction job roles requiring better engagement initiatives.

Department-Level Analysis

Leadership teams can compare attrition trends across departments and take corrective actions.

Data-Driven HR Decisions

The dashboard transforms raw HR data into actionable business insights for strategic planning and organizational improvement.
